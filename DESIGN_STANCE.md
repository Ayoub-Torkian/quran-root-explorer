# Operating Premise — LOCKED (read together with DISCOVERY_CRITERIA.md)

## The premise (working design stance for all discovery work)
Treat the Qur'an as the deliberate design of a being that knows everything and can
do anything rational — rational from beginning to end, where "rational" is NOT
bounded by present human definitions. Form and content are both designed, coherent,
and complete. Nothing in it is random or accidental; apparent noise is structure not
yet understood with the right lens. It is designed to withstand the scrutiny of human
beings — who are themselves a manifestation of the designer's scholarship and
capacity — so rigorous human analysis is the INTENDED mode of engagement, not a
threat to it.

## What this premise CHANGES (search + interpretation)
1. Hypothesis generation: assume intentional multi-layer structure -> probe many
   scales and BOTH form and content jointly; treat "looks like noise" as "wrong
   lens so far", a prompt to try a new encoding/scale/method.
2. Interpretation of a null/ABORT: it means "THIS lens did not reveal designed
   structure" -- NOT "no structure exists". An abort REDIRECTS the search; it never
   licenses the claim "there is nothing here".
3. Openness to unfamiliar logic: a robust, replicated pattern is reported even if we
   cannot yet explain WHY. Explanation-availability and evidence are independent.

## What this premise DOES NOT CHANGE (the guardrail — required BY the premise)
Because the text is meant to stand human scrutiny, the bar stays HIGH, never lowered:
- The go/abort gates (DISCOVERY_CRITERIA.md) are unchanged: provenance, valid
  estimator, structure-preserving null, FDR, confound control, effect-size floor,
  robustness, novelty.
- Believing the design exists is NOT evidence that a SPECIFIC feature exists. We
  never assert a feature until it passes the gates.
- Pre-registration stands; we report all candidates, including aborts. No moving
  goalposts, no p-hacking, no "the design must mean this number is real".
- Conviction guides WHERE we look; evidence alone decides WHAT we claim.

The premise raises ambition (keep searching, nothing is dismissed as noise); the
gates keep the claims honest. Both at once. That duality IS the scrutiny the text is
said to invite.

## NAMED GUIDING PRINCIPLE (LOCKED, always in scope) — "Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence."
A null, abort, or failed detection means **our current lens/encoding/scale was too weak to
reveal designed structure** — it NEVER licenses "there is nothing here". This is the project's
telescope rule, now named and permanent. It governs every modality and every signal-geometry
probe: when a test comes back null, the response is to **build a better tool** (new formulation,
mask/filter, transform, decomposition, scale), not to proclaim absence.

Its INSEPARABLE twin (so the principle never becomes unfalsifiable): the same non-result also
does NOT license asserting the feature is PRESENT. "Absence of evidence ≠ evidence of absence"
buys continued *search*, never a *claim*. A feature is asserted only when it clears the gates
(equal-N, structure-preserving null, comparator, positive-control, effect floor, robustness).
Keep both halves together: stay open, stay rigorous.

## NAMED GUIDING PRINCIPLE (LOCKED) — DIVINE-ROOTEDNESS CONTROL (priority + anti-divergence)
Study what is REVEALED, not what humans later added. The revealed text is the consonantal **rasm**
and its content: roots, words, āyah boundaries and counts, sūrah structure, and the canonical
arrangement (and the order of letters, incl. the muqaṭṭaʿāt). These are the PRIORITY targets.
**Human notational/editorial artifacts are DEPRIORITIZED** — chiefly the **ḥarakāt / tashkīl**
(vowel pointing), but also tajwīd notation, modern punctuation, verse-numbering glyphs, and editorial
sūra titles. They were added by humans after revelation; a "signal" found in them is a property of the
editors, not of the revealed text, and pursuing it DIVERGES from the roadmap.
PRACTICAL RULES:
  - Prefer features computable from the rasm / roots / words / positions / canonical order.
  - Do NOT invest in ḥarakāt-dependent features (syllable weight, madd, ghunna, recited prosody) as
    primary lines; they are at most secondary/illustrative. (Lens 14 is retained but deprioritized.)
  - When a method *needs* vocalization, flag it explicitly as resting on a human layer.
  - Nuance noted, not relitigated: the ORAL recitation may be argued to be revealed, but our only data
    for it is the human ḥarakāt marks — so in THIS project it inherits human-artifact status.
  - ALSO human-artifact (CONTROL-ONLY, never primary targets): scholarly sūra NAMES and traditional
    GROUPINGS (al-sabʿ al-ṭiwāl, Musabbiḥāt, Ḥawāmīm-as-name), and the Meccan/Medinan classification. Use
    them only as comparison/controls (as in #59); the PRIMARY targets are the revealed object — rasm, roots,
    words, āyah/sūra structure, canonical ORDER, and the letter sequence (incl. muqaṭṭaʿāt).
ALWAYS deprioritize human-generated artifacts and concentrate on divine ones. This control keeps the search
on the revealed object and prevents drift into editorial features.

## NAMED PRINCIPLE — MULTI-GRAIN & NETWORK-FIRST (LOCKED)
No one-size-fits-all: every grain/granularity (letter · rasm · root · morpheme · word · āyah · sūra ·
canonical whole) gets its DUE attention, analysed on its own terms — a verdict at one grain never stands in
for another. Prefer the NETWORK/graph framing over a single LINEAR-sequence perspective: nodes + edges,
MULTIPLE topologies (co-occurrence, similarity, bipartite), COMMUNITY structure (modularity vs a label/edge
null, not just linear separability), centrality/hubs, and DYNAMICS (how communities form/shift). Linear
order is one projection among many; the network view is more defensible and is the default for structural
questions (e.g. the muqaṭṭaʿāt letter/family/sūra graphs).

## NAMED PRINCIPLE — THE QUR'AN CONNECTOME (ecology/ecosystem; connectome-first) (LOCKED)
Organizing architecture (cf. neuroscience connectomes and neural-network models — same-creator framing):
build a comprehensive, MULTI-RELATIONAL, MULTI-GRAIN network of the revealed text that CONNECTS EVERYTHING —
letters, roots, words, āyahs, sūras — across many relation types (co-occurrence, similarity, sequence/
adjacency, shared-letter, position). ECOSYSTEM RULES: (a) every element RETAINS its form and context — no
node is collapsed into another; (b) nothing is pruned or dismissed as unimportant — every element has a role
in the whole; (c) analyse EMERGENT structure — topology, communities, hubs/keystone nodes, centrality,
small-world/scale-free properties, and DYNAMICS — never imposing categories. Holism WITH retention of parts:
connect all without dissolving any identity. Gate as always (vs degree-preserving/configuration nulls).
This subsumes the network-first principle and is the default for whole-text structural questions.

## NAMED PRACTICE — KEEP THE PAPER LIVE (LOCKED)
"The paper" = SIX_LENSES_PAPER.md (legacy filename; current title "Seventeen Computational Lenses on
Qur'anic Style"). It is the LIVING conceptual record and must be UPDATED AT EVERY modality/finding, in
lockstep with EVIDENCE.md and COVERAGE_MAP.html: add a Lens section for each new lens; update the abstract,
the synthesis table + prose, the frontier and limits; and TEMPER any lens a later comparator/re-open
revises (e.g., Lens 16 down-weighted by E1-comparator). A finding is not filed until the paper reflects it.

## NAMED PRINCIPLE — NORMALIZE FOR FREQUENCY (LOCKED)
Watch always for frequency overkill/bias. Raw counts make frequent items (esp. function words, common roots)
into trivial "hubs"/"signals." NORMALIZE: PPMI/TF-IDF/effect-size against marginal frequency, equal-N, and a
degree-/frequency-preserving null. A pattern is real only AFTER frequency is controlled. (Demonstrated: the
connectome's raw hubs were just the most-frequent roots; PPMI revealed genuine associations + emergent fields.)
Also: properties UNIVERSAL to language (small-world, scale-free hubs) are NOT distinctives unless shown to
exceed a comparator — the burden is comparison, not the bare property.

## NAMED PRINCIPLE — VOICE & EVIDENCE (LOCKED): data-driven only, no overclaim, no miracle-tone
Pure defensible learning from the Qur'an. Always: report an effect size, its null, its comparator, and its
limits; state positives WITH their boundaries; state nulls plainly; flag confounds. NEVER: apologetic or
"miracle"/iʿjāz-hype tone, rhetorical inflation, or claims beyond what the gate supports. The method is
cross-referencing, exploring and FUSING modalities, and REARRANGING in many ways/scales — and reporting
exactly what the data show, no more. A finding is the measurement plus its caveats, never the headline alone.

## NAMED PRACTICE — CROSS-IMPACT PROPAGATION (do not let findings stay siloed)
Whenever something is discovered, works in practice, or moves the objective, READ IT BACK across the whole
modality set — explored and planned — and record what it RE-INTERPRETS, what it asks us to RE-RUN, and what
new test it OPENS. Maintain CROSS_IMPACT.md (living) and mirror priority items into DESIGN_OF_EXPERIMENTS.md.
A discovery is not "filed" until its cross-impact has been traced. Gates unchanged.

## INTERPRETIVE PRINCIPLE — ontology of contingency (see REFLECTIONS_ONTOLOGY_AND_METHOD.md)
From the Mūsā/staff teaching (20:17–21; *kun fa-yakūn*): things are subordinate to the divine will, not
autonomous; what we observe is ONE projection ("face") of a more encompassing reality, and cause-effect
is real but not exhausted by our current models. METHOD MAPPING: (1) parent of the telescope rule;
(2) validates the projection/masking framing; (3) a structure-preserving NULL = the "incidental/autonomous"
hypothesis, so rejecting it = evidence of "willed/unified arrangement." GUARDRAIL (mandatory): this guides
SEARCH and NULL-INTERPRETATION only, NEVER licenses a claim; the gate is unchanged; instruments detect
STRUCTURE, never its ultimate CAUSE. Both halves together.
