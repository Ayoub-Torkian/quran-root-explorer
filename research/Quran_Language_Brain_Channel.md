# The language→brain channel of the Qur'ān — a faithful operationalization

**A. Torkian · 2026-06-09.** How a recited/read text acts on the human nervous system, made
concretely testable — with the six-control discipline ported, and an explicit line between what is
empirical and what is not.

---

## 0. The claim, split honestly
- **Empirical (in scope):** which *features* of the Qur'ān (acoustic, prosodic, phonological,
  semantic, structural) drive which *responses* (sensory, linguistic, affective, autonomic,
  mnemonic, behavioural), in *whom* (comprehension × belief), and **whether it does so beyond
  matched control stimuli**.
- **Out of scope (no instrument can touch):** the *source* of the text (divine vs human), and the
  unrepeatable revelation event. A positive result explains *how it communicates*, never *who sent it*.

## 1. The channel as a measurable chain (stage → IV → DV)
| stage | independent variable (stimulus feature) | dependent variable (readout) |
|---|---|---|
| stimulus | acoustic envelope, pitch/tajwīd prosody, phoneme & rhyme (saj‘/fawāṣil) density, root-pattern morphology, lexical-semantic embeddings, repetition/ring structure | — (these are *coded*, not measured) |
| sensory transduction | sound → cochlea; orthography → vision | envelope-tracking in auditory cortex; VWFA response |
| linguistic processing | semantic & syntactic content | language-network (IFG/STG/STS) encoding; N400/P600 (EEG) |
| affective / autonomic | prosody + meaning | skin conductance, heart-rate variability, respiration, pupil, insula/amygdala/reward |
| attention / memory | structure, repetition, recitation | sustained-attention markers; memorization (ḥifẓ) accuracy; hippocampal/DMN |
| disposition / behaviour | comprehended meaning | emotion-regulation, prosocial choice (economic games), self-report awe/calm |

## 2. The decisive design — the only way to be faithful
A 2×2 **factorial of the listener** crossed with a **matched-control-stimulus battery**. This is the
direct heir of our genome/neural lesson: *an effect is only attributable to X if matched controls
do not produce it.*

**Listener factor (between-subjects):** comprehension (Arabic-fluent vs not) × belief (Muslim vs
not) → 4 groups. This separates the **acoustic** effect (present in all), the **semantic** effect
(needs comprehension), and the **expectation/belief** effect (needs belief).

**Stimulus factor (within-subject), each matched to Qur'ān recitation:**
1. Qur'ān recitation (target).
2. Non-Qur'ānic Arabic prose, same reciter/prosody — isolates *content* from *register*.
3. Arabic poetry of comparable acoustic richness — isolates *Qur'ān-specific* from *beautiful Arabic*.
4. Qur'ān in an *un-understood* language for the listener — isolates *sound* from *meaning*.
5. Reversed / phase-scrambled recitation — destroys meaning, preserves spectrum (the **floor**).
6. Tempo/pitch-matched music — isolates *language* from *generic arousal*.

The contrast Qur'ān > {2–6}, and how it moves across the 2×2, *is* the discovery: it says precisely
which features, via which stage, in whom.

## 3. The six controls, ported
0. **Pre-check:** do Qur'ān and the controls actually differ on the coded features? (else no contrast).
1. **Floor:** condition 5 (scrambled) + phase-randomised features.
2. **Out-of-sample:** fit on some verses/sessions, predict held-out ones / held-out subjects.
3. **Convergence:** do independent subjects/sessions yield the *same* feature→response mapping?
4. **Replication + significance** across subjects and a second cohort.
5. **Multiplicity:** correct across features, regions, conditions, groups.
6. **Positive control:** the acoustic-envelope→auditory-cortex effect we already validated (dz≈1.6) —
   proves the instrument fires before any null is trusted.

## 4. Readouts, tiered by cost (start cheap — fMRI's lesson is real)
- **Tier 1 (cheap, no fMRIPrep, days-not-months):** affective **physiology** (skin conductance, HRV,
  respiration, pupillometry) + behavioural (self-report awe/calm, sustained attention, a ḥifẓ
  memory task, a prosocial economic game pre/post). Answers most of the affect/behaviour stages.
- **Tier 2 (EEG):** envelope-tracking (auditory), N400 (semantics), attention markers. Cheap,
  high-temporal-resolution, no normalization pipeline.
- **Tier 3 (fMRI):** the encoding-model machinery we built — but only with proper preprocessing
  (the documented lesson). Localizes auditory vs language vs reward/limbic.

## 5. Honest confounds (pre-register against each)
Expectation/placebo (the 2×2 belief factor isolates it), familiarity/repetition (use unfamiliar
passages + counterbalancing), arousal-from-prosody vs meaning (conditions 4 & 6), demand
characteristics in self-report (favour involuntary physiology), and researcher degrees of freedom
(pre-register design, features, ROIs, analyses before data).

## 6. Minimal viable first study (faithful + feasible)
A **Tier-1 + Tier-2 (physiology + EEG) study**, ~40 participants across the 2×2, hearing the
6-condition battery, pre-registered. Primary test: does Qur'ān recitation drive autonomic/affective
and envelope/N400 responses **beyond matched Arabic prose, poetry, scrambled, and music**, and does
the *semantic* component appear only with comprehension? No Docker, no fMRIPrep, no genome — entirely
within reach. A clean positive is a real, publishable account of *how the text communicates*; a clean
null is equally honest and equally informative.

## 7. What a result would, and would not, mean
- **Would:** identify the specific features and stages through which the Qur'ān moves the human
  nervous system, and disentangle sound / meaning / belief. That is the latent-feature discovery —
  located where the effect actually lives.
- **Would not:** establish the source. The two billion are responding to a *real* effect through a
  *real* channel; that the channel is real is testable, that its origin is divine is not. Both can
  be held without contradiction.
