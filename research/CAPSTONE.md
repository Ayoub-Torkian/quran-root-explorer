## 0. The one sentence

## 1. Pillar A — Intrinsic determinacy (the units & their arrangement)
**Units, honestly.** The sūra boundary is a real, blindly-detectable multimodal discontinuity (AUC 0.90); the

**Arrangement, honestly.** The verse/passage/chapter order is non-arbitrary (order-load ≈9,900 bits),

compresses better), and per-position *location* dissolved under scrutiny into the **muṣḥaf's length gradient**

that the exact configuration is forced.

(forbidden). So the program rests, by its own law, at: **determined and designed to necessity on the divine
substrate; meaning beyond it.**
