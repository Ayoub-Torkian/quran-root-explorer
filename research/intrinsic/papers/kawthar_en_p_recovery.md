## 6.9 Recovery of the tradition: a scorecard

The literature of §3 is bound by our ONE LAW not to serve as *evidence*; but it can serve as a *comparator*. If the internal method is doing real work, its output should line up with what a millennium of reading has held — and where it cannot adjudicate, it should say so. {{T12}} scores each principal reading: does the corpus-only method **recover** it (as meaning), and can it **adjudicate** it (as referent)?

*{{T12}}. Recovery scorecard. "Meaning" = does the internal method reach the same sense? "Referent" = can the method single this out as the thing meant? (Per §6.7, referent-adjudication is outside an internal instrument by design.)*

| Reading of *al-Kawthar* | Principal source(s) | Meaning recovered? | Referent adjudicated? |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Abundant good" (*al-khayr al-kathīr*) | al-Ṭabarī (← Ibn ʿAbbās); al-Tafsīr al-Muyassar | **Yes** — the genus of *k-th-r*, blessing vs amassing (§5.2) | n/a (this *is* the genus) |
| A river / fountain (*ḥawḍ*) in paradise | majority hadith (~30 reports); Ibn Kathīr | Consistent (an instance of the genus) | **No** — not distinguishable by distribution |
| Prophethood; the Qurʾān; abundant followers | al-Rāzī and others (surveyed) | Consistent (instances) | No |
| Abundant / enduring **progeny** | al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī, *al-Mīzān* | **Yes** — answers *abtar*; coheres with the severance axis (§5.3) | No |
| **Fāṭima** and her enduring line | *Tasnīm* (Jawādī Āmulī); standard Shia reading | Consistent — the progeny-referent made concrete | No — a *referent* claim, beyond distribution (§6.7) |
| (The "occasion": a taunt by al-ʿĀṣ / Kaʿb) | *asbāb al-nuzūl* reports | **Not used** — the reading is recovered *without* it (the paper's point) | n/a |

Two things follow [INFERRED, ~85%]. First, the internal method **recovers the shared *meaning*** that the whole tradition agrees on — *al-Kawthar* as God-given super-abundance, set against amassing (*takāthur*) and severance (*abtar*) — and it does so *without* the occasion-report. That is the honest positive result: not a new meaning, but an independent, corpus-internal *recovery* of the old one. Second, exactly where the tradition itself **disagrees** — river vs progeny vs Fāṭima vs prophethood — the method **declines to adjudicate**, because that is a *referent* question requiring co-reference grounding, not distribution (§6.7). The method's reach and its limit thus map cleanly onto the tradition's agreement and its disagreement: it confirms the consensus *meaning* and is silent on the disputed *referent*. This is what makes "recovery of tradition" a measured comparison here, rather than decoration.
