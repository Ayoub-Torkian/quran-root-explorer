# 10. Unresolved issues and future research

This study closes more questions than it opens about *meaning*, but it leaves several genuinely open problems, most of them about the **instrument** rather than the text. We list them in the order of how much they would change the picture.

**Resolved by the robustness checks added in §5.29.** Seven of the problems originally listed here have since been addressed and are retained only as a record of how they were settled: the **independent-encoder test** (§5.29.1 — the relational web reproduces ≈14× above chance for attested roots, while the *batr*–*shaniʾ* edge is demoted to a single-verse artifact); the **PPMI-inflation / graph-robustness** caveat (§5.29.1, count-sensitive measures applied); the **falsifiability of the thematic readings** (§5.29.2 — a count-matched fittingness null: real, but a *general* short-sūra property); the **regulative-voice null** (§5.29.2 — unsupported, p = 0.99); **generalising "long elaborates short"** (§5.29.5 — *not* systematic, p = 1.0; the true partner is al-Ḍuḥā); **cross-text generalisation** (§5.29.3 — dense singularity is particular: al-Kawthar and al-Ikhlāṣ carry it, al-ʿAṣr and al-Naṣr do not); and the **explicit openness gradient** (§5.29.4 — a [HUMAN CONSTRUCT] ranking; al-Kawthar 1st of 114; singularity ≠ openness). The items that remain genuinely open are below.

**The remaining excluded instrument — morphology.** The independent-encoder lever has now been pulled (§5.29.1); the one tool still bracketed is a **morphology / pattern (wazn) tagger**, which would let sense be resolved by form rather than by neighbour-context alone — distinguishing, e.g., *qalb* "heart" from *yanqalib* "to turn" — and so test the form-aware sense unit directly. Until that is done, the sense-resolution behind the relational findings remains coarser than ideal.

**The open referent of *kawthar*.** Our method fixes the *sense* of *kawthar* (abundant, continuing, God-given) but deliberately leaves its *referent* open (§3.4). Adjudicating among the river, the abundant good, and the Fāṭima-progeny readings is not a distributional problem at all; it requires **referent-grounded (co-reference) modelling** — linking expressions to the entities they denote — which the present co-occurrence substrate cannot supply. A co-reference layer is the natural next instrument, and the heart-anatomy precedent in this project suggests it must be partly hand-grounded. The openness ranking of §5.29.4 *localises* this problem (al-Kawthar is the corpus's most under-determined sūra) but, by the *taʾwīl*-humble limit, cannot *resolve* it.

**Finer grains and other arrangements.** The relational-law and coherence results were measured mainly at sūra and āyah grain on the canonical order; re-testing at **morpheme grain** and on the **revelation-order** arrangement (a legitimate "divine-alt" indexing) would show how much of the structure is arrangement-specific.

**A referent-grounded semantic substrate.** The project's standing proposal — rebuild the web on nodes defined as *(morphological-form sense) × (co-reference-merged referent)* rather than raw roots, then re-run cohesion, attraction and self-interpretation on *that* substrate — remains the most promising single redesign, and would bear directly on several of the above.

**The second-person address map needs full referent-grounding (*-ka* and *-ta*).** The personal-vs-office result (§5.25) rests on a referent-blind corpus-wide proxy for *-ka* (validated by hand only for the top three sūras) and on a qualitative treatment of the verb-suffix *-ta*. A complete and defensible address map requires a **referent-tagged second-person index across all 114 sūras** — labelling, for every *-ka* and *-ta*, which addressee is meant (the Prophet, Moses, Adam, the disbelievers, a generic "you"), **and which function** (verb-object "you", possessive "your", or benefactive *laka* "for you") — to confirm that al-Kawthar is the global maximum of Prophet-personal address. This is necessarily part hand-grounded, in the manner of the heart-anatomy co-reference work.

**The principled limit beyond the openness gradient.** §5.29.4 builds the openness gradient as a reproducible [HUMAN CONSTRUCT] score; what it cannot do is principled, not instrumental: because *taʾwīl* is the real referent-terminus (and partly unfolds in time, 7:53), no synchronic instrument can *output the taʾwīl* of an open word such as *kawthar*; localising and ranking candidate referents is the most an honest instrument may do. This is the *taʾwīl*-humble boundary of the whole program, and it will not be removed by a better learner.

**The residual theodicy.** §6.6 answers the charge that an unearned, differential gift is *unjust* (no right is denied; judgement is on use, capacity-scaled; the gift instantiates a universal defence-law). It does **not** close the deepest question — *why* capacities to believe and to strive are unequally distributed in the first place. This is a theological-philosophical problem, not a corpus measurement, and by its nature lies outside the instrument; we record it as an honest, standing remainder.

**Verification of the cited exegetical positions.** The positions attributed to Ṭabāṭabāʾī (*al-Mīzān*) and Jawādī Āmulī (*Tasnīm*) on 53:39 and on *taʾwīl* (§6.6, §6.7) are summarised as scholarly *positions* and flagged; the exact passages should be checked against the primary texts, and the two *waqf* readings of 3:7 attributed to their named proponents, before any of this is relied upon in a published version.
