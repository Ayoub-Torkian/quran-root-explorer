## Operational definition of "necessary" (pre-committed — this is the win line; kills the genuine→null cycle)
A configuration is **NECESSARY** iff there exists an **intrinsic criterion C** (measured on the **rasm**,
DIVINE-DEFAULT arrangement, proper null) under which the **actual configuration is the UNIQUE strict optimum** —
every alternative scores strictly worse.
- **LENGTH necessary** = each sūra's actual end is the unique cut where C is satisfied; moving the end by ±k
  breaks C. Metric: % of sūras whose length is forced by C (local optimum of C), vs an arbitrary-cut null.
- **ORDER necessary** = the canonical permutation is the **strict global optimum** of C: **0%** of rearrangements
  improve it (today ≈10% do), and it is unique vs a permutation null + a held-out check.
- **GATE:** "proven" only at the strict optimum (improving-moves → ~0) with a proper null. Anything short =
  "strongly constrained," recorded as such, never reported as necessity.
