Where are we in the Qur'ān discovery landscape?

A map, not a network. Left→right = scale (a single letter up to the whole book). Bottom→top = from FORM to MEANING (sound & rhythm at the bottom, the arrangement in the middle, meaning/discourse at the top). Green = charted territory (where our 22 passed features live). Pale/striped = frontier — thin or unmapped. The ★ is the north star (define the Sūra & Āyah, and why this arrangement). The dot is where we are now. The orange line is the journey — where we started, where we went, and where we stand (waypoints 1→6, explained below).

The journey — where we started → where we are (the orange line)

1 · Started outside (pre-2026-06-07): the "is it distinctive?" phase — two-books genome, neural-coding, external comparator sweeps.   2 · The pivot (2026-06-07): drop external evidence, go fully internal — the One Law.   3 · Form base: verse-length waves, 1/f rhythm, rhyme runs (L01–07).   4 · Network & constellation: self-echo, structural twins, the sūra fingerprint (L08–09, L21).   5 · The conquest (Phases B–D, 2026-06-09/10): the order is determined and the sūra is defined to necessity (L11–26).   6 · Now (2026-06-11): the āyah opened on the rasm (~85), per-sūra necessity came back null — and we're pressed against the comprehension wall, where the form surface yields diminishing returns. The dashed green arrow is the only way on: cross into meaning, which the rasm can't reach alone.

Where we're off-balance

Over-charted (lots of converging evidence): the whole FORM side — sound, rhythm, and especially ORDER / ARRANGEMENT and SŪRA BOUNDARIES. We know the text's outer architecture, from the verse up to the whole book, extremely well. Six features each on order and on sūra-definition.

Under-charted (thin or empty): MEANING / content — almost nothing direct, only proxies; this is the single biggest gap. The small end (word weak, letter detached). The Āyah, only just opened (~85). And sufficiency in general — we have many "this pattern exists" results, few "this is enough to force the unit" results.

Direction check — are we on course or astray? Not astray: form was the right first conquest, and it's nearly complete. But the north star sits in the thin region — meaning and sufficiency — and the last three ≥90 attempts on the form surface landed ~85, null, null. So continued drilling of finer FORM features is the "going-astray" risk (diminishing returns). The two honest ways forward both leave the charted green: go small (the word/letter rung), or cross the comprehension wall toward meaning — which on the rasm is walled, and only opens if you deliberately admit a second substrate (the recited/diacritic layer) as a declared, separate track.

One line: we have mapped how the Qur'ān is arranged very thoroughly, and barely touched what it means — and meaning is exactly what the definitions still need.
