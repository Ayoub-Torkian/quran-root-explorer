# Sign and Sorcery — الآية والسحر
### A data-driven deep dive: siḥr vs the divine sign — and why the Qurʾān has no word "muʿjiza"

**The frame.** The question was posed as: *what is siḥr, how much, what types, why, what does it point to, is it
encoding anything, word- or character-level* — the same lens we put on the muqaṭṭaʿāt, now on **سحر (sorcery)** and
its relation to **معجزة (miracle)**. The first finding reframes the whole question.

**Finding 0 — "muʿjiza" is not a Qurʾānic word. [TEXT · MEASURED]** The noun **معجزة / معجزات occurs 0×**. Its
root عجز appears 26× but always means **to frustrate / escape / be unable** (*مُعْجِزِين* "you cannot thwart [God]",
9×) — never "a miracle." The theological term *muʿjiza* ("that which renders opponents *incapable* of producing
the like") is a **sound but post-Qurʾānic** derivation from the challenge (*taḥaddī*) verses. **[REPORT], not [TEXT].**
The Qurʾān's own vocabulary for the wonder that authenticates a prophet is **آية "sign" (root 382×)**, **بيّنة /
مبين "clear proof" (523×)**, **برهان "proof" (8×)**. So the real Qurʾānic pair is **āyah (sign) ↔ siḥr (sorcery)**.

**Sense-resolution.** سحر (63×) is almost entirely *sorcery*; the homograph **سَحَر "pre-dawn"** accounts for only
**أسحار** (3:17, 51:18, "those who seek forgiveness at dawn") and *bi-saḥar* (54:34) — ~3 tokens, set aside here.

---

## 1. What siḥr is, and how much  [TEXT · MEASURED]

**63 occurrences**, ~60 in the sorcery sense, across **27 sūras**, but **heavily clustered in the Moses–Pharaoh
confrontation**: sūras **7 (7×), 10 (7×), 20 (10×), 26 (10×)** hold **34** of them. Forms: **سِحْر** (magic, 29) ·
**ساحِر** (magician, 12) · **سَحَرة** (the magicians, 8) · **مسحور** (bewitched, 3) · **سَحّار** (arch-sorcerer,
7:112) · **ساحران / سِحران** (two sorcerers / two magics, 20:63, 28:48). It is a **word-level** concept (unlike the
muqaṭṭaʿāt, which were character-level) — the vocabulary of a *contest of signs*.

## 2. What kind of thing it is — siḥr is mostly what deniers SAY  [TEXT · MEASURED]

The single most telling measure: **44 of 59** sorcery-verses carry the root **قول "to say"** — siḥr is
overwhelmingly a **speech-act of denial**, the label thrown at a sign, not a neutral description. The recurring
formula is **سِحْرٌ مُبِين "manifest sorcery," 9×**, and it is hurled at **every** messenger:
- **Moses** — 10:76, 27:13; **Jesus** — 5:110, 61:6; **Muhammad** — 6:7, 46:7, 37:15; and the Book itself,
  *"إِنْ هَٰذَا إِلَّا سِحْرٌ يُؤْثَر"* (74:24).
And the mirror-accusation **مسحور "bewitched"** is aimed at the prophet's *person*: *"إِنِّي لَأَظُنُّكَ يَا مُوسَىٰ
مَسْحُورًا"* (17:101), *"إِن تَتَّبِعُونَ إِلَّا رَجُلًا مَّسْحُورًا"* (17:47, 25:8).

The irony is exact: the sign is a **بيّنة (clear proof)**; the denier calls it **سِحْرٌ مُبِين (clear sorcery)** —
*same adjective "clear/mubīn," opposite verdict.* Belief and denial look at one extraordinary event and read it
two opposite ways. **siḥr is the denier's name for the āyah.**

## 3. The types of siḥr in the text  [TEXT]

1. **Taught sorcery** — learned from the *shayāṭīn*, the Bābil / Hārūt–Mārūt episode (**2:102**); the text calls
   learning it **kufr** (*"فَلَا تَكْفُر"*) and says it **harms, does not benefit**.
2. **The magicians' performance** — Pharaoh's *saḥara* cast ropes and staffs (7, 10, 20, 26): a **contest** staged
   against Moses' sign.
3. **The accusation-label** — *"this is sorcery"* / *"you are bewitched"* (§2): the largest class.

## 4. The differentiators — how the Qurʾān tells the sign from the sorcery  [TEXT · MEASURED]

Both are extraordinary and eye-catching; the text does **not** distinguish them by spectacle but by four things:

- **Source.** Sorcery is taught by devils and against God's leave (2:102); the sign is *bi-idhni-llāh*, "by God's
  leave."
- **Ontology — illusion vs reality.** Sorcery works on the **eyes**: *"سَحَرُوا أَعْيُنَ النَّاسِ"* (7:116);
  *"يُخَيَّلُ إِلَيْهِ مِن سِحْرِهِمْ أَنَّهَا تَسْعَىٰ"* (20:66 — it only *appears* to move). The sign is **ḥaqq**,
  real. Sorcery is *takhyīl* (seeming); the sign *is*.
- **Outcome.** *"وَلَا يُفْلِحُ السَّاحِرُ حَيْثُ أَتَىٰ"* (20:69 — the sorcerer never prospers); *"إِنَّ اللَّهَ
  سَيُبْطِلُهُ"* (10:81 — God will nullify it); Moses' staff *"تَلْقَفُ مَا يَأْفِكُونَ"* (7:117 — swallows their
  fabrication). The counterfeit is consumed by the real.
- **Recognition by the experts.** The decisive tell: **the magicians themselves prostrate.** *"فَأُلْقِيَ السَّحَرَةُ
  سَاجِدِين"* (26:46; also 7:120, 20:70) — the very specialists in siḥr instantly know Moses' sign is **not** siḥr,
  and believe. Expertise in the counterfeit is what certifies the genuine.

## 5. Structural type — a *counterfeit / adversarial mirror* (a confusable pair with a hidden differentiator)

In the atlas typology (axis · field · partition · ladder · frozen pair), siḥr↔āyah is a distinct shape: a
**counterfeit pair.** Unlike the light/dark **axis** (clean opposites), the two are **built to be confusable** —
both extraordinary, both "clear" — and the concept's whole work is supplying the **hidden differentiators** (§4)
that tell the real from the mimic. The meaning lives not in one pole, nor in the contrast, but in the
**criterion that separates look-alikes.**

## 6. What it points to · is it "encoding" anything · word or character level

- **Points to:** the **epistemology of the sign** — *how a true sign is known.* siḥr exists in the text to be the
  foil that defines the āyah by negation (real vs illusory, prospering vs nullified, God-sourced vs devil-taught).
- **Encoding:** not a cipher. It **encodes a stance** — siḥr is the denier's verdict; āyah/bayyina the believer's.
  The same event, two readings; the text adjudicates by source/ontology/outcome.
- **Level:** **word-level** (a lexical–narrative concept), the opposite pole from the muqaṭṭaʿāt's character-level
  letters.

## 6b. The taḥaddī — the standing challenge that closes the loop  [TEXT · MEASURED]

The accusation *"he forged it"* (أَمْ يَقُولُونَ افْتَرَاهُ, **10:38 · 11:13**) and *"he said it himself"* (تَقَوَّلَهُ,
**52:33**) is answered with one move: **then produce the like.** The challenge (taḥaddī) is issued at four scopes —
the whole Qurʾān, even humans and jinn combined (*لَا يَأْتُونَ بِمِثْلِهِ … ظَهِيرًا*, **17:88**); **ten** forged
sūras (**11:13**); **one** sūra (**2:23 · 10:38**); merely **a discourse** like it (**52:34**) — plus the variant
"bring a better Book from God" (**28:49**). Recurring condition: *إِن كُنتُمْ صَادِقِين* ("if you are truthful").

Two measured features make it the pivot of the whole sign/sorcery question:
1. **A falsifiable, standing prediction.** Uniquely, **2:24** adds *فَإِن لَّمْ تَفْعَلُوا وَلَن تَفْعَلُوا* — "and
   you will **never**." Other prophets' signs were one-time events; the Qurʾān's sign is **the text itself**,
   permanently present and openly challengeable.
2. **Where the missing word comes from.** The challenge asserts the opponents' **عَجْز (inability)** — *لا يأتون
   بمثله* — and it is precisely from that عجز that later theology coined **iʿjāz / muʿjiza**. The noun is absent from
   the Qurʾān (Finding 0); the *idea* is **derived** from these verses.

So the loop closes: **siḥr / iftirāʾ** (the denier's "sorcery / forgery") → **taḥaddī** ("then match it") →
**عَجْز** (they cannot) → the standing **āyah** that authenticates. *(Sequencing note **[REPORT]**: the popular
"de-escalating challenge, whole → ten → one" is not cleanly borne out by revelation order — 17:88 whole, then 10:38
one, then 11:13 ten; 2:23–24 latest. Measured: the four scopes, the humans+jinn maximum, the never-clause.)*

## 6c. The forms of the sign — every element as the Maker's instrument  [TEXT · MEASURED]

The āyah takes more than one form: the **evidentiary sign** given to a messenger (Moses' staff; Ṣāliḥ's she-camel,
"made visible," 17:59), the **standing sign** of the Book (§6b), and — when the sign is denied — the
**punishment-sign**, a sudden and total event. Across the punished nations, **every element of creation is drawn on
as God's instrument** — *"to God belong the hosts (junūd) of the heavens and the earth"* (48:4·7; 74:31):

| People | Element | Mechanism | Verse |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nūḥ's people | water | flood / drowning — الطوفان · أغرقنا | 29:14 · 7:64 |
| ʿĀd | wind | howling wind — رِيحٌ صَرْصَرٌ عَاتِيَة | 69:6 · 41:16 · 54:19 |
| Thamūd | sky / earth | the Cry · bolt · quake — الصَّيْحَة · الصَّاعِقَة · الرَّجْفَة | 11:67 · 41:17 · 7:78 |
| Pharaoh | sea | drowned — أَغْرَقْنَاهُم فِي الْيَمّ | 7:136 · 10:90 |
| Qārūn | earth | swallowed — فَخَسَفْنَا بِهِ وَبِدَارِهِ الْأَرْض | 28:81 |
| Lūṭ's people | sky + earth | stones + overturning — حِجَارَةٍ مِّن سِجِّيل · عَالِيَهَا سَافِلَهَا | 11:82 · 15:74 |
| Madyan | sky / earth | the Cry · the quake — الصَّيْحَة · الرَّجْفَة | 11:94 · 7:91 |

**Historical tag:** the events are **[TEXT]** (Qurʾānic narration); identifying each people with a specific site/date
is **[REPORT]** and not asserted. Shared, measured signature: **sudden/unawares** (وَهُمْ لَا يَشْعُرُونَ, 16:45),
**total** (a whole qawm), **timed** (لِكُلِّ أُمَّةٍ أَجَلٌ, 7:34).

**Why no punishment-sign today? [TEXT]** *"وَمَا مَنَعَنَا أَن نُّرْسِلَ بِالْآيَاتِ إِلَّا أَن كَذَّبَ بِهَا
الْأَوَّلُونَ"* (17:59) — the demanded punishment-signs were **withheld** from the final community, because the ancient
pattern was sign → denial → annihilation. The sign **changed form, not substance**: the **standing sign** (the Book)
+ **deferred judgment** (the reckoning is the Hour) — *"We send not the signs except to warn"* (تَخْوِيفًا). The
**pattern (sunnat Allāh) is unchanged** — فَلَن تَجِدَ لِسُنَّةِ اللَّهِ تَبْدِيلًا (35:43); only its **mode** differs.
[INFERRED] So the answer to "do we still have muʿjiza?" is: **yes, in a changed form** — the Book as the standing
sign, and judgment deferred rather than immediate.

## 7. Honesty ledger

- **[TEXT · MEASURED]:** معجزة 0× / عجز = "frustrate" (26×); āyah 382, bayyina 523, burhān 8; siḥr 63 (~60 sorcery),
  clustered 34/63 in sūras 7·10·20·26; 44/59 with قول; سحر مبين 9× (Moses, Jesus, Muhammad); the four differentiator
  verses (2:102, 7:116, 20:66, 20:69, 10:81, 7:117); magicians prostrate (26:46, 7:120, 20:70).
- **[INFERRED]:** that siḥr is the *counterfeit/mirror* of the sign and that its role is to define the āyah by
  negation; the "confusable-pair" structural type.
- **[REPORT] (flagged):** that *muʿjiza* is the mature theological packaging of the taḥaddī/āyah material — sound,
  but not a Qurʾānic word; the Bābil/Hārūt–Mārūt backstory beyond the verse's own words is external.

**Confidence:** the lexical facts (muʿjiza absent; siḥr counts and the accusation pattern) ~99% — counting. The
"counterfeit-mirror" reading ~80% — strongly supported by the four differentiators and the prostration, but a
reading.
