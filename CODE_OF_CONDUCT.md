# Code of Conduct

This is a global, open project that studies a text sacred to many people. Everyone is welcome to
take part, regardless of background, belief, or nationality. To keep it a place where good work can
happen, we ask everyone to follow a few simple commitments.

## Our standards
- **Be respectful and constructive.** Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- **Keep it scholarly.** This project is evidence-first and measured. Avoid polemic, proselytizing,
  or attacks — in any direction — about the subject matter.
- **Assume good faith.** Contributors come from many cultures and languages; read generously and
  explain clearly.
- **Credit others' work.** Acknowledge sources and contributions honestly.
- **No harassment, discrimination, or abuse** of any kind.

## Scope
This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any related
communication.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these standards, and may restrict
participation for repeated or serious violations. To report a concern, contact a maintainer privately
(see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for private contact routes).

*Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant; kept short and plain on purpose.*
