الشبكة الباطنية · The inner-self network

One reality with two co-present orientations — دنیا (the near, root دنو=draw near; the passing) and آخرة (the lasting/real life, الحیوان). By what it knows and does, the self becomes دنیوی or اخروی; the غطاء (veil) over perception is present in life and lifts when sight sharpens (50:22). Outcome = kawthar (joined to the lasting → crosses) or abtar (clinging to the near → cut off). Hover any node or edge for its Qurʾānic anchor. Edges: green=toward the lasting/openness · red=toward the near/severance · gold=feedback · grey=structure.
