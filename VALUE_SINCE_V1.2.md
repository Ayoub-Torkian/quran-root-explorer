## 1. Four findings that survived everything

**① The Architecture of Return (#42, ~+3σ).** The Qur'an's defining structural signature: locally *less* continuous than ordinary prose, but globally it RETURNS — re-expressing the same content across long ranges far more than any comparator (recurrence pairs: cosine 0.68 but edit-similarity only 0.27 — return without refrain). Sharpened by #70: narrative anchors (Mūsā 135×, Firʿawn 74×…) recur *continuously* across revelation time — return is a standing mode, not a phase.

**② The fāṣila system (Lens 17).** Three converging properties at the verse-end: (a) heavy ending-repetition, cross-text distinctive — 0.18 surface-grain vs saj' 0.04, ordinary 0.10, poetry 0.02 (#63, corrected #76); (b) the ending *predicts its āyah's body content* — root z=+11.3, morphology z=+12.1 (#62); (c) referent-general — the fit holds whether the seal names God, believers, or punishment (#77, 15/16 classes). Saj' rhymes; it does not do this.

**③ The muqaṭṭaʿāt (Lens 15, sui generis).** Exactly half the alphabet (14/28) · spatially clustered in the muṣḥaf (Moran I=+0.54, robust under nuzūl order) · internally order-disciplined (no letter-pair ever reversed across 29 openings; near-ceiling consistency, top ~0.04% of all possible orders) · modular letter-combinatorics (reuse z=−12.6) · deployed in revelation-time waves (z≈−5). Content is NOT letter-organized (honest negative). No comparator text has anything like this layer.

**④ Temporal deployment (Lens 18).** The seal system has a TIME dimension: a 2×2 typology of rate-waves × content-re-aiming. Some seals are "campaigns" whose meaning is re-aimed across the mission (يعلمون: creation-ignorance → scripture-ignorance; اليم: past nations → covenant-breach; عليم: knowledge-contest → legal audit → purification). Canonical arrangement is order-typed (#57/#72): adjacent sūras cohere beyond length (z=+3.1), themes sort differently in canonical vs nuzūl order.

## 2. What was honestly killed or corrected (the credibility layer)

Wavelet-on-length: retracted (rhyme-channel artifact). #63 headline: corrected 0.28 → 0.18 (lemma-grain audit). Abjadī key-match: re-framed as human historical frame. Plus ~12 modalities filed register-level/null (semantic fields, dependency syntax, discourse macro-structure, phonosemantics, directionality…). **The positives are believable because the negatives are filed beside them.**
